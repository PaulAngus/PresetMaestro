On 2026-10-03 this table was superseded by all 336 factual ID/name pairs read
directly from the connected FM9 running firmware 12.00, using read-only amp-name
table queries. The historical filename is retained. Raw replies and unchanged
device-state checks are in docs/catalog/fm9-12-device-roster.json. Both index
lookup and browser use this complete FM9 12.00 table. Other device families and
firmware versions require independently confirmed rosters.

search-index table/section excerpts; its live page returned HTTP 403. Historical
manual details can conflict with current references and are not silently adopted.
Ampdex transcribes Clayton Welch's guide, which draws on Yek, the Wiki and forum.
Those two community references are NOT independent evidence. Their explanatory
prose, guide pages and images are not redistributed in these runtime files.
Only factual product identities and original review notes are included.
The review joins 329 original CSV entries to device-confirmed numeric IDs.
The combined MK IV LEAD/RHYTHM heading and Fox ODS Deep remain unjoined; the
device's selectable Lead and Mid names have separate mappings. Runtime family
data covers all 336 observed names in 162 family/original groups.
No CSV row position or Ampdex entry number is used as a DSP model ID. Matching
names alone does not verify numeric identity or firmware availability; the
device observation establishes these separately for FM9 12.00. Physical
custom amps, virtual originals and schematic interpretations remain distinct.
No prefix-based runtime grouping is used.
